# Steve's Rules for Performance

## Steve's **_Golden Rule_** of performance

Not doing stuff is way faster than doing stuff

## Steve's **_Other Rule_** of performance

Feeling fast is pretty much as good as actually being fast.

## I am going to show you this at the end

- if you can solve a problem with how shape your **_component hierarchy_** or **_state_**- do that **_first_**

- **_Memoization_** is a solid strategy **_only_** if the const of checking pays for itself with the time you save rendering.

- Using the **_Suspense API_** to progressively load your application is a **_good idea_**. and, more good stuff will come soon.

- The **_Transition API_** is there for you when you're **_really_** in a pickle.
